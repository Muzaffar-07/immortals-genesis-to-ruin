The Glass Choir is a downloadable Windows development build of Immortals.

- Play as Ena or Aro in an authored industrial facility.
- Animated combos, timed parries, evasions, blink strike, gravity pull and time fracture.
- Human guards, Dr. Mara Vale, Director Voss, evidence and branching conversations.
- Seven linked mission stages, consequential choices, a phased boss and ending variants.
- Full HD default, optional 4K supersampling, recorded spatial effects and music.
- Open-source game code and credited redistributable assets; works offline.

Extract the whole ZIP and run `Immortals.exe`. Keep the PCK beside it. Keyboard and
mouse required. `Launch-Compatibility.cmd` offers reduced OpenGL effects.

This is a development slice, not a finished AAA or PS5 release. Dialogue is text,
enemy tactics are limited, and 4K performance depends on your GPU.

Controls: WASD/mouse, Shift sprint, C jump, LMB combo, RMB block/parry, Space evade,
Q blink, R gravity, F time fracture, Tab lock, E interact, M journal, Escape pause.
