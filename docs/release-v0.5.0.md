# The Glass Choir — voiced cinematic update

The Blackwater mission now includes spoken conversations and skippable story scenes.
Choose Ena or Aro and hear their replies to Mara and Director Voss, including the
consequences of the witness and archive decisions.

- More detailed principal characters with blinking and audio-reactive mouth movement.
- Revised skin, eye and clothing materials, softer bloom and cinematic depth of field.
- 27 offline generated voice recordings (about 115 seconds across both heroes).
- Directed arrival, witness, choice, villain and ending sequences. Space/Escape skips.
- Separate jab, cross and hook animations with timed hits and buffered input.
- V performs a heavy front kick; Space uses an animated evade. Short lunges close range.
- GPU-compressed character textures reduce memory use; repeated guards use smaller maps.

Extract the entire Windows ZIP and run **Immortals.exe**. Keep its `.pck` file beside
it. Full HD is the default. An OpenGL compatibility launcher is included. The build
is unsigned, runs offline and needs no account.

This is an open-source development slice, not a finished AAA/PS5 production. Voices
are synthetic; facial motion is amplitude driven rather than performance capture.
4K supersampling is optional and does not guarantee 4K source assets or a frame rate.
Character, environment, animation, music and voice-generation licenses are included.

Validation: automated mission checks cover both heroes and branching endings,
combo/heavy attacks, defensive powers, voice assets and facial activation, directed
scenes, skip/cancel restoration, pause, death and checkpoint restart. Assisted story
tests do not establish difficulty balance or broad hardware compatibility.

The exported Windows build passed with zero integration errors. Forward+ rendering
completed the title, gameplay, combat, hero close-up, Mara conversation and Voss
reveal captures on a GTX 1650 Ti without renderer errors. ZIP integrity was checked.
